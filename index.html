Stacks — the few combinations that genuinely work as one workflow

Most products here are best on their own. These are the exceptions: bundles where the products share a customer and a workflow. Each one says exactly why it goes together — if the reason ever stops being true, the stack goes away.

The philosophy

Connect ideas only where it makes sense. A portfolio of independent products with a few natural bundles beats one artificial mega-product. So:

  • Independent by default — every product solves one problem for one kind of user, and works fully on its own.
  • Clusters are for browsing — they group products that serve similar users, nothing more.
  • Stacks must earn it — a bundle only exists if the products share a real workflow and a real customer.
  • Free to run — no paid services, no required API keys. AI works locally; an OpenAI key is optional polish, never a requirement.
  • Local-first — your data stays in your browser or on your machine.
  • Tested — every repo ships with smoke + end-to-end tests, all green.
  • Open — each card links straight to its public repo.
